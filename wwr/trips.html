Our Rafting Trips

Río Cauca

A relaxed 4-hour float through green canyon scenery, perfect for first-timers and families. Gentle Class II rapids give you a taste of the thrill, and our guides stop along the way for swimming and photos. At $55 per person, it's our friendliest introduction to whitewater.

Río Magdalena

Colombia's most famous river brings 6 hours of steady Class III rapids mixed with calm stretches where you can rest and take in the landscape. It's a great step up for anyone with a little paddling experience. The $75 price includes lunch on the riverbank.

Río Atrato

A 2-day expedition deep into rainforest country, with Class III-IV rapids by day and camping on the riverbank by night. Expect wildlife, dense jungle, and plenty of paddling challenges along the way. This $220 trip is for adventurers who are comfortable in fast water.

Río Negro

Five hours of varied water that suits both beginners and intermediate paddlers, with Class II-III rapids and scenic forested banks. Our guides adjust the route to your group's comfort level, so everyone gets the right amount of excitement. The $65 trip is great value for mixed-experience groups.

Ríos del Orinoco

Our ultimate 3-day expedition through the powerful rivers of the Orinoco basin, with Class IV rapids and remote camping under the stars. Previous whitewater experience is required, and you'll be rewarded with some of the most challenging runs we offer. The trip costs $340 and includes meals, gear, and expert guides.
